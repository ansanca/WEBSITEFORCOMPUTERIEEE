Our Mission & Vision

Pioneering technology culture on campus and bridging the gap between academic theory and industry practice.

Who We Are

The IEEE Computer Society Student Branch Chapter at MBITS is the student arm of the world's leading professional organization for computing. We connect our campus to a global network of over 400,000 members across 160+ countries.

The Objective

To empower students with cutting-edge technical skills, industry connections, and leadership opportunities through impactful events, hackathons, and community-driven learning.

The Future

To be a nationally recognized student chapter that shapes the next generation of computing professionals, researchers, and technical innovators.
